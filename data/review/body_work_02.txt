#80|Cosy Critters & Creepy Crawlies|Adds adorable and abstruse atmospheric animals, accentuating aesthetics. A client mod. Does not need to be installed on the server. All effects can be individually disabled or configured: use command /cosycritters or the mod list menu. Birds Birds flock together in the sky and occasionally land on blocks, favoring locations high up. Birds will take off and scatter when disturbed by a nearby entity such as a vehicle, mob, or projectile. Tiny Spiders Appear from within cobwebs , crawling out along the walls, floors, and ceilings. Moths Attracted to lights. Will surround any light emitting block left exposed to the sky.
